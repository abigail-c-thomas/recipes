This *patina uersatilis*, or "easily turned-out dish", appears to be akin to a modern omelette. An almost identical *patina uersatilis uice dulci* ("sweet") appears at [*Apicius*](/sources/apicius.html) 4.2.16 and differs only in the presence of a little wine. The use of pepper and [fish sauce](/ingredients/garum.html) in what is essentially a dessert may seem odd to modern tastes but is typical of other Roman sweet dishes. It can serve as either a dessert or a sweet appetizer.

{% capture ingredients %}
* 40g mixture of hazelnuts, almonds, and/or walnuts
* 20g pine nuts
* Generous ground pepper
* 3 eggs
* 60ml milk
* 10ml [fish sauce](/ingredients/garum.html)
* 10ml olive oil
* 15ml honey, plus extra for serving
{% endcapture %}

{% capture instructions %}
1. Place the nuts into a frying pan and toast for a few minutes. Tip into a mortar and pestle and grind into a coarse powder.
2. Place the remaining ingredients into a mixing bowl and whisk until smooth.
3. Pour the egg mixture into the frying pan along with the nuts, and cook gently. Flip or finish under the broiler to cook the top. Serve with additional drizzled honey.
{% endcapture %}
