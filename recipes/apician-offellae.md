Named after [Apicius](/sources/apicius.html), the famous Roman gourmand, this is a more refined version of the popular meat dish [*ofellae*](/dishes/ofellae.html). Tiger nuts, actually tubers of the plant *Cyperus esculentus*, appear occasionally in classical cuisine and are now probably most famous as the basis of the Spanish drink *horchata*.

{% capture ingredients %}
* 750 g pork belly
* 150 ml [raisin wine](/ingredients/passum.html)
* 45 ml [fish sauce](/ingredients/garum.html)
* 15 ml olive oil
* 10 g tiger nuts, crushed
* 10 g cumin
* 2.5 g lovage
* Ground black pepper
{% endcapture %}

{% capture instructions %}
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
2. Roast the pork belly in the oven until well done, about 1 hour.
3. Once pork belly is cooked, remove from the oven and slice into 3 cm × 3 cm chunks.
4. Place the pork along with the remaining ingredients into a saucepan.
5. Bring to a simmer and continue cooking until the sauce has mostly evaporated and clings to the meat.
6. Remove the meat from the saucepan, sprinkle with pepper, and serve.
{% endcapture %}

{% capture notes %}
* If you can't find tiger nuts, you can substitute 10 g of almond meal.
{% endcapture %}
