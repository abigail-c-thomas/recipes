This recipe from [Vinidarius](/sources/vinidarius.html) represents the continued popularity of [*ofellae*](/dishes/ofellae.html) in late Roman cuisine. It is the only surviving recipe from the ancient Mediterranean which uses cardamom, an otherwise little known spice from southern India used occasionally in medicines. The use of cardamom, along with the other eastern spices [asafoetida](/ingredients/asafoetida.html), ginger, and cardamom, reflects the continued persistence of trade networks even during the decline of the Western Empire.

{% capture ingredients %}
* 700g bone-in pork spare ribs, chopped into bite-sized pieces
* 60ml [fish sauce](/ingredients/garum.html)
* 1tsp ground ginger
* 0.75tsp ground cardamom
* 0.5tsp [asafoetida](/ingredients/asafoetida.html)
* 300ml water
{% endcapture %}

{% capture instructions %}
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
2. Place the pork onto a baking tray, place in the oven, and bake for 40 minutes.
3. Place the pork into a small dutch oven along with the [fish sauce](/ingredients/garum.html), spices, and 300ml of water.
4. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 30 minutes or until the pork is falling off the bone.
5. Remove the spare ribs from the cooking broth and serve.
{% endcapture %}
